Human Resources Tool

A responsive, privacy-centric HR SaaS application optimizing leave requests and role management through modern, user-friendly interfaces.

Role: UX/UI Designer Timeline: 6 Months
View Live Project

Context & challenge

The design challenge was to simplify routine HR tasks such as leave requests, personal-information updates, and role management across desktop and mobile.

Approach & key decisions

As UX/UI designer, I established reusable interface patterns, simplified the leave-request concept to two steps, and designed a personalized dashboard that surfaces relevant employee tasks.

Tools & Technologies

Figma Design Systems Interaction Design SaaS UX

Outcome & reflection

The reusable component approach makes the prototype more consistent and easier to extend. Delivery-speed and support-ticket improvements have not been measured, so the case study treats them as future validation goals.

Evidence & next validation

The interface capture and live prototype show the current design direction. Research sample details, support-ticket baselines, and production adoption data have not been published and are not presented as outcomes.
