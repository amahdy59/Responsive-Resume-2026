HR Management Tool

All-in-one workforce operations platform streamlining leave approvals, performance reviews, and department shift scheduling.

Role Lead UX/UI Designer
Timeline 4 Months
Platform Web Application (SaaS)
Status Production Deployed
Visit Live Project
Open in New Tab

01 01 Context & Challenge

Employees and HR personnel faced fragmented paper forms, confusing email approval chains, and lack of visibility into available leave balances.

02 02 Approach & Key Decisions

Engineered modular dashboards with self-service request portals, instant role-based approval routing, and accessible interactive calendar timelines.

Key Design Decision

Engineered a 1-click batch review modal that allows department heads to approve recurring team schedule adjustments in seconds.

03 03 Tools & Technologies

Figma Bilingual RTL/LTR Design Tokens SaaS UX User Flows Accessibility

04 04 Outcome & Impact

Designed the self-service and approval flow to reduce delays and improve request-status clarity; verified production data is still required to quantify the operational impact.

05 05 Evidence & Next Validation

System actively used by multiple corporate teams. Planned additions include AI-assisted talent onboarding recommendations.

06 06 Project Provenance & Learnings

Ownership

Ahmed Mahdy owned the UX design, information architecture, visual direction, content, and final project decisions.

Professional Perspective

The work draws on his UX practice and background in eLearning and video authoring to simplify information, sequence interactions, and build a clear narrative.

AI-Assisted Implementation

Ahmed translated the Figma designs into a live project with assistance from Antigravity, Codex, and Claude agents, then reviewed, refined, and tested the implementation.

Special Learning

Special learning: modeling role-based workflows and approval states while reviewing AI-generated implementation for accessibility and clarity.
