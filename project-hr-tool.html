Human Resources Tool

A responsive, privacy-centric HR SaaS application optimizing leave requests and role management through modern, user-friendly interfaces.

Role: UX/UI Designer Timeline: 6 Months
View Live Project

Context & challenge

The legacy HR software was notoriously difficult to navigate, leading to a high volume of support tickets for simple tasks like requesting PTO or updating personal information. The interface was outdated and not optimized for mobile.

Approach & key decisions

I spearheaded a complete redesign, establishing a scalable design system and standardizing components. I streamlined the leave request flow into a simple 2-step process and introduced a personalized employee dashboard to surface relevant tasks immediately.

Tools & Technologies

Figma Design Systems Interaction Design SaaS UX

Outcome & reflection

Creating a comprehensive design system from scratch pays massive dividends. While it slowed down the initial weeks of the project, it exponentially increased our velocity when rolling out new features later in the timeline.
