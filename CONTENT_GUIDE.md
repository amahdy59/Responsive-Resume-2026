# Portfolio content guide

`data/projects.json` is the canonical source for project titles, platforms, case-study URLs, and live URLs in both languages. Update it together with visible copy; `npm run check` rejects drift across homepage cards, case studies, metadata, structured data, translations, and preview links.

Case studies must use the same evidence order: context, problem, role and ownership, team, constraints, process and decisions, rejected or failed ideas, delivered solution, evidence, limitations, and next validation step.

Classify each project as shipped, pilot, prototype, or concept. Every quantitative outcome needs a source, measurement period, sample, and Ahmed's contribution. If evidence is unavailable, describe the observable deliverable and say that an outcome was not measured; never substitute a plausible number. Client-sensitive information should be generalized without implying evidence that cannot be shown.

Image captions should explain what the reader is seeing and why it matters. Alt text should convey the same information needed to understand the surrounding argument, without repeating the caption verbatim.

Keep one concise site-level AI disclosure in About. Case studies should use their final section for project-specific learning instead of repeating generic ownership, professional-background, or tool disclosures.
