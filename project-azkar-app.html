Azkar App – Daily Fortress

A serene, accessible Islamic daily remembrance and prayer times web application designed for spiritual consistency and focus.

Role Lead UX/UI Designer
Timeline 2024–2025
Platform Responsive Web App / PWA
Status Live Production
Visit Live App

01 Context & Spiritual Habit Loop

Modern Muslims often struggle to maintain daily remembrance routines (Morning, Evening, and Sleep Azkar) amidst cluttered notifications and overly complex apps. The goal was to build a distraction-free, beautifully atmospheric experience that turns daily remembrance into an effortless, grounding habit.

02 Information Architecture & Daily Wird Flow

Structured around a clear daily wird progression (0/3 checkpoints for Morning, Evening, and Sleep remembrance) and accurate prayer-time tracking with post-prayer remembrance status.

Key UX Innovation: Dynamic Time-Window Prompts

The dashboard intelligently foregrounds the relevant remembrance based on current solar time (e.g., Evening Azkar window active between Asr and Maghrib) with an estimated completion timer (~11 mins) and counter mode toggle (Complete vs. Abbreviated).

03 Tools & Technologies

Figma Islamic UX Design Bilingual EN/AR RTL Midnight Dark Mode PWA & Offline First WCAG 2.2 AAA

04 Accessible Design & Midnight Theme

Crafted with a specialized Midnight theme tailored for low-light morning and evening recitation. Typography adheres to Arabic calligraphic legibility rules with generous line-heights and high-contrast counters.

05 Outcome & Community Engagement

Launched as an open, accessible web app with zero advertisements and fast offline caching, resulting in high daily retention and positive user feedback on streak motivation and prayer synchronization.
