A Data-Driven LEGO Explorer

Interactive Tableau dashboard exploring 18,000+ LEGO sets across historical themes and complexity tiers.

Role Data Analyst & Visualizer
Timeline 2 Months
Platform Tableau Public Visualization
Status Published Live
View Tableau Showcase

01 Context & Challenge

A massive 50-year dataset of over 18,000 LEGO sets presented overwhelming tabular data that obscured critical trends in theme longevity, piece counts, and price inflation.

02 Approach & Key Decisions

Structured a dynamic Tableau visual narrative with cross-filtering, historical price normalization, and interactive age-bracket distribution charts.

Data Storytelling Decision

Designed intuitive visual hierarchy with high-contrast color encoding to allow non-technical collectors to instantly uncover pricing and rarity trends.

03 Tools & Technologies

Tableau Data Storytelling Statistical Analysis Information Design Excel ETL

04 Outcome & Impact

Delivered an engaging public visualization showcased on Maven Analytics, enabling users to explore decades of toy manufacturing history effortlessly.

05 Evidence & Next Validation

Showcase project published live on Maven Analytics with interactive public workbook downloads.
