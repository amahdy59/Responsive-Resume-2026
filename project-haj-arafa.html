01 Context & Challenge

The design brief focused on simplifying deeply nested mobile categories and a lengthy checkout flow that required account creation.

User problem

The brief called for easier product discovery and a shorter checkout journey. These needs informed the concept; they are not findings from a reported user study.

Business problem

The brief identified checkout friction as a business risk. Production abandonment and repeat-purchase data were not available for this concept.

Design goal

Design a mobile-first shopping journey with search-first navigation and a shorter guest checkout.

02 Approach & Key Decisions

Mapped the intended shopping journey, designed search-first navigation, guest checkout, and prominent 44px touch targets informed by relevant WCAG 2.2 criteria.

Key Design Decision

Consolidated five interface stages into one accordion-based checkout concept with address autofill and a clear cost breakdown.

04 Outcome & Impact

Delivered a streamlined shopping experience that improves product discovery, simplifies checkout, and builds customer trust.

Search-first navigation

The implementation gives search and curated categories prominent positions. Findability remains a question for usability testing.

Streamlined checkout

The proposed checkout shortens the purchase path. Its effect on conversion has not yet been measured.

Responsive implementation

Built mobile-first with keyboard interaction, visible focus states, and responsive layouts informed by WCAG 2.2.

05 Results & Validation

Live store application is available online. Conversion rate analytics and cart abandonment data will be monitored over upcoming quarterly releases.

06 Ownership & Learning
Ownership

Ahmed Mahdy owned the UX design, information architecture, visual direction, content, and final project decisions.

Professional Perspective

The work draws on his UX practice and background in eLearning and video authoring to simplify information, sequence interactions, and build a clear narrative.

AI-Assisted Implementation

Ahmed translated the Figma designs into a live project with assistance from Antigravity, Codex, and Claude agents, then reviewed, refined, and tested the implementation.

Special Learning

Special learning: shaping a concise responsive commerce journey and directing AI agents to implement navigation and checkout states faithfully.

Designed with accessibility, WCAG 2.2, and performance in mind.
