Haj Arafa App

Mobile-first e-commerce interface showcasing search-first navigation and express 2-step checkout.

Role Lead UX Designer
Timeline 3 Months
Platform Mobile-First Web App
Status Production Live
Visit Live Project
Open in New Tab

01 01 Context & Challenge

Mobile shoppers were experiencing high bounce rates due to deeply nested category menus and a lengthy, multi-step checkout process with forced account creation.

02 02 Approach & Key Decisions

Conducted user journeys to design search-first mobile navigation, guest checkout, and prominent 44px touch targets, designed and tested against relevant WCAG 2.2 criteria.

Key Design Decision

Reduced checkout friction from 5 disjointed steps down to a single streamlined accordion flow with instant address autofill and clear cost breakdowns.

03 03 Tools & Technologies

Figma User Research Wireframing Prototyping E-Commerce UX WCAG 2.2 informed

04 04 Outcome & Impact

Created a frictionless shopping experience that significantly shortens the path from product discovery to order confirmation.

05 05 Evidence & Next Validation

Live store application is available online. Conversion rate analytics and cart abandonment data will be monitored over upcoming quarterly releases.

06 06 Project Provenance & Learnings

Ownership

Ahmed Mahdy owned the UX design, information architecture, visual direction, content, and final project decisions.

Professional Perspective

The work draws on his UX practice and background in eLearning and video authoring to simplify information, sequence interactions, and build a clear narrative.

AI-Assisted Implementation

Ahmed translated the Figma designs into a live project with assistance from Antigravity, Codex, and Claude agents, then reviewed, refined, and tested the implementation.

Special Learning

Special learning: shaping a concise responsive commerce journey and directing AI agents to implement navigation and checkout states faithfully.
