Haj Arafa App

An intuitive, accessible e-commerce experience designed with responsive layouts and seamless navigation to maximize user conversion and engagement.

Role: Lead UX Designer Timeline: 3 Months
View Live Project

Context & challenge

The design challenge was to make product discovery and checkout easier on mobile without overwhelming shoppers with dense navigation or a long sequence of decisions.

Approach & key decisions

As lead UX designer, I shaped a search-first navigation model, guest checkout, mobile-sized touch targets, and a step-by-step checkout flow. These decisions aim to shorten the path to purchase; conversion impact has not yet been verified with published analytics.

Tools & Technologies

Figma User Research Wireframing Prototyping WCAG 2.2

Outcome & reflection

The available implementation demonstrates the mobile-first flow and accordion checkout direction. The next validation step is a documented usability study followed by funnel analytics; no conversion improvement is claimed on this page.

Evidence & next validation

The interface capture and live implementation are the available evidence. Research participant counts, baseline funnel data, and post-launch conversion metrics have not been published, so they are not presented as outcomes.
