Sales Performance Dashboard

CRM sales performance dashboard visualizing revenue, conversion rates, and quota attainment.

Role Data Analyst & Dashboard Designer
Timeline 1 Month
Platform Google Sheets / BI Dashboard
Status Published Live
View Sales Showcase

01 Context & Challenge

Sales management lacked real-time visibility into quarterly pipeline health, regional rep quotas, and product-tier conversion funnels.

02 Approach & Key Decisions

Engineered an automated Google Sheets analytics engine with dynamic KPI summaries, trend sparklines, and automated rep ranking calculations.

Analytical UI Decision

Combined modular KPI scorecards with dynamic date range selectors to eliminate manual monthly report compiling.

03 Tools & Technologies

Google Sheets Data Modeling KPI Dashboards Formulas & Macros Revenue Analytics

04 Outcome & Impact

Empowered leadership to identify conversion bottlenecks and reallocate sales focus to high-margin revenue streams.

05 Evidence & Next Validation

Live interactive model published on Maven Showcase with full performance calculation schemas.
