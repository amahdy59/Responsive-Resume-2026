Sales Performance Dashboard

CRM sales dashboard built in Google Sheets for tracking quarterly team performance through chart-based visualization.

Role Data Visualizer & BI Designer
Timeline 1 Month
Platform Google Sheets & Analytics
Status Published Live
View Live Project

01 Context & Challenge

Sales directors were spending hours manually aggregating rep performance across scattered spreadsheets without real-time visibility into quota attainment or pipeline velocity.

02 Approach & Key Decisions

Built an automated, formulas-driven Google Sheets CRM dashboard with visual KPI summary cards, progress bars, and lead source conversion funnels.

Key Design Decision Designed conditional formatting logic and automated status indicators that immediately flag stalled pipeline opportunities requiring sales management intervention.

03 Tools & Technologies

Google Sheets Data Visualization Sales Analytics Dashboard Architecture KPI Tracking

04 Outcome & Impact

Empowered team leadership to conduct weekly pipeline reviews in half the time with instant visibility into individual and team quota progress.

05 Evidence & Next Validation

Working spreadsheet template and visual dashboard are available on Maven Showcase.
